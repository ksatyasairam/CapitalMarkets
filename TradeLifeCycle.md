# Trade Life Cycle (TLC) Overview:

## 1. Pre-Trade
**Preparation**: Involves client onboarding, risk assessment, and setting up necessary legal agreements.<Br>
**Order Placement**: Investors place orders through brokers or trading platforms.<Br>
## 2. Trade Execution
**Order Matching**: Orders are matched with counterparties on exchanges or over-the-counter markets.<Br>
**Confirmation**: Trade details are confirmed and communicated to both parties.<Br>
## 3. Clearing
**Verification**: Trade details are verified, and obligations are determined.<Br>
**Netting**: Multiple trades are netted to reduce the number of transactions.<Br>
## 4. Settlement
**Transfer of Assets**: Actual transfer of securities and funds between parties.<Br>
**Finalization**: Ensuring all obligations are met and the trade is completed.<Br>
## 5. Post-Trade
**Reconciliation**: Ensuring records match and resolving discrepancies.<Br>
**Risk Management**: Ongoing management of positions and risks.<Br>

This cycle ensures that trades are processed efficiently and accurately, maintaining market integrity and transparency.
