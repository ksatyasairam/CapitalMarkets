# Trade Life Cycle (TLC) Overview:

## 1. Pre-Trade
**Client Onboarding**: Involves setting up accounts, conducting due diligence, and completing necessary legal agreements.<Br>
**Order Placement**: Investors place orders through brokers or trading platforms. This includes specifying the type of security, quantity, and price.<Br>

## 2. Trade Execution
**Order Matching**: Orders are matched with counterparties on exchanges or over-the-counter markets. This involves algorithms and trading systems to ensure efficient matching.<Br>
**Trade Confirmation**: Details of the trade are confirmed and communicated to both parties, ensuring accuracy.<Br>

## 3. Clearing
**Trade Verification**: Ensuring that trade details are accurate and obligations are determined. This step involves clearing houses that act as intermediaries.<Br>
**Netting**: Multiple trades are netted to reduce the number of transactions, minimizing risk and operational costs.<Br>

## 4. Settlement
**Transfer of Assets**: Actual transfer of securities and funds between parties. This step ensures that both parties fulfill their obligations.<Br>
**Finalization**: Ensuring all obligations are met and the trade is completed. This involves updating records and confirming the completion of the transaction.<Br>

## 5. Post-Trade
**Reconciliation**: Ensuring that all records match and resolving any discrepancies. This step is crucial for maintaining accuracy and integrity.<Br>
**Risk Management**: Ongoing management of positions and risks, including monitoring market conditions and adjusting strategies as needed.<Br>
**Reporting and Compliance**: Generating reports for regulatory compliance and internal auditing purposes.<Br>

## 6. Archiving
**Record Keeping**: Storing all trade-related documents and data for future reference and regulatory requirements.<Br>
**Audit Trails**: Maintaining detailed records to ensure transparency and accountability in the trading process.<Br>

This comprehensive cycle ensures that trades are processed efficiently and accurately, maintaining market integrity and transparency.
